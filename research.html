My work explores how AI, immersive learning, and cybersecurity practice can support technical education and workforce readiness. This page distinguishes completed academic writing and working tools from ongoing inquiry and planned defensive research.

Completed academic work

COMPLETED COURSEWORK · MAY 2026

Adaptive and immersive AI for workforce readiness

Authored “Integrating Adaptive and Immersive Artificial Intelligence to Improve Student Success and Workforce Readiness in Higher Education” for South College’s EDU6501: Accountability and Research in Leadership.

The paper examines adaptive learning, immersive environments, faculty preparation, and organizational readiness for AI adoption in technical programs.

Academic literature-based coursework; not a peer-reviewed publication or a completed intervention study.

COMPLETED COURSEWORK · MAY 2026

Ethical leadership in AI and cybersecurity programs

Authored “Ethical Leadership and Initiative Implementation in Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence Programs” for South College’s EDU6500: Theory & Practice in Leadership.

The analysis considers academic integrity, security risk, accountability, and collaboration across educational and technical leadership roles.

Academic analysis and recommendations; not evidence that the proposed governance changes have been implemented.

ONGOING RESEARCH INTEREST

Adaptive, immersive, and explainable AI

Exploring how personalized learning, realistic simulations, and understandable AI feedback can support technical workforce readiness. This direction builds on my academic writing and instructional design work.

ONGOING RESEARCH INTEREST

Responsible AI in technical education

Examining how educators can incorporate AI into technical practice while preserving authentic skill demonstration, academic integrity, privacy, and human oversight.

Planned defensive security research

Future areas of investigation include AI-assisted security operations, AI agent permission boundaries and prompt-injection resilience, and risk-based vulnerability management in authorized environments.

These topics are research directions. Completed experiments, benchmarks, and published findings are not yet presented here.
